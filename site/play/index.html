JUNIPER FM-1

a six-voice polysynth for the M-VAVE FM-1 · · play it in your browser

This is the real firmware, run unchanged on an emulated FM-1 by fm1-emu. Press Start in the emulator, then play with your computer keyboard or a MIDI keyboard. Chrome or Edge work best.

Open the player full size Install on your FM-1 Source on GitHub

Sound stuttering? The emulator starts at a lower clock to suit slower computers: raise Clock under the emulator if your computer keeps up, or lower VOICES on the GLO page. The first start downloads the firmware (600 KB).

Playing it

On your keyboard
A … 'play C4 to F5 (black keys on the row above)
Z Xoctave down / up
SpacePLAY: chord memory (play a chord, then each key plays it)
Knobsdrag or scroll a knob on the panel; PRESET or SELECT changes the patch, ALGO jumps eight

The pages (click the panel's buttons; press again for the next page)

ButtonPages
HOMEthe patch: FREQ, RES, VCF ENV, CHORUS
EDITDCO › DCO 2 (range, PWM mode, saw, pulse) › VOICE (per-voice detune, vibrato, PWM)
SELVCF › VCF 2 (key follow, envelope polarity, HPF)
ENVENV › ENV 2 (the filter's own) › VCA
LFOLFO › LFO 2 (sync, detune LFO, drift)
FXCHORUS: chorus, HPF, level
GLOVOICE MODE › VELOCITY / BEND › SCALE / CLOCK › MIDI
ARParpeggiator off › on › hold, and its page (order, range, rate, gate)
SAVEstore the patch (saves stay in this browser)

128 factory patches: pads, arpeggios (A15 to A27 start the arpeggiator), basses, brass, subs, leads, strings, plucks, stabs and chords (B53 to B64 play a chord from one key). A polysynth inspired by the Juno-106. Unofficial: not affiliated with or endorsed by Roland or M-VAVE. The emulator is Joakim Eriksson's; JUNIPER FM-1 is MPL-2.0.
